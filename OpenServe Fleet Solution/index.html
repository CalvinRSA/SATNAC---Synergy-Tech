Your AI-powered geospatial tool for transitioning Openserve’s fleet to Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs). Plan sustainable deployments, optimize charging infrastructure, and ensure uninterrupted service across South Africa.

About Openserve

Openserve, a Telkom subsidiary, is South Africa’s leading telecom infrastructure provider, connecting over 1 million homes with its fibre network. With a fleet of 1,000-2,000 vehicles, we’re driving sustainability through NEV adoption.

Transforming our fleet for a greener future.

Powering connectivity across all nine provinces.

The Challenge: Limited EV Infrastructure

With over 500 charging stations in 2025, South Africa’s EV network is growing but uneven. Urban areas thrive, while rural “charging deserts” risk service disruptions for Openserve’s fleet.

Eskom’s pilot stations signal progress.

Urban hubs like Cape Town lead in infrastructure.

Our Solution: AI-Powered Geospatial Analysis

The EVD-CVM web app integrates Python, GPS APIs (Open Charge Map), and OpenAI to classify regions for BEV deployment, recommend charger placements, and visualize plans via interactive GIS maps.

Interactive maps for strategic planning.

Budget: R350,000 - R500,000
Timeline: 6 months
Tools: Python, OpenAI, GPS APIs

About the Project

Developed with:

  • Python: Backend logic with GeoPandas, Shapely.
  • GPS API: Open Charge Map for real-time charger data.

Aligned with Openserve’s sustainability goals and national NEV policies, targeting 15-20% cost savings.
