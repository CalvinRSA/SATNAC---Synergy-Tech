About Openserve

Openserve, a Telkom subsidiary, is South Africa's leading telecom infrastructure provider, connecting over 1 million homes with its fibre network. With a fleet of 1,000-2,000 vehicles, we're driving sustainability through NEV adoption.

Transforming our fleet for a greener future.

Powering connectivity across all nine provinces.

The Challenge: Limited EV Infrastructure

With over 500 charging stations in 2025, South Africa's EV network is growing but uneven. Urban areas thrive, while rural "charging deserts" risk service disruptions for Openserve's fleet.

Eskom's pilot stations signal progress.
